# Safety Policy - Working at Height

1. A safety harness is mandatory for any work above 2 m when no guardrail is installed.
2. Scaffolding and fall-protection equipment must be inspected every day before the first shift.
3. All work at height must stop when the wind speed is above 45 km/h.
4. Tools must be secured with lanyards when used above ground level.
5. Any near miss must be reported within 24 hours.
