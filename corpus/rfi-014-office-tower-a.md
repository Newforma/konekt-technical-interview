# RFI-014 - Fire-rated glazing, Office Tower A

Question from contractor: which fire rating is required for the glazing on levels 30 to 35, next to the evacuation stairs?

Answer from the architect (2024-08-12): the glazing next to evacuation stairs must have a fire resistance of 90 minutes. Standard 60-minute glazing is not accepted for these levels. The change affects the curtain wall order and is the reason for the supplier re-quote.

Status: closed.
