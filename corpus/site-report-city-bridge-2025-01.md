# Site Report - City Bridge Renovation - January 2025

Work is paused since 20 January because of freezing temperatures. Welding and painting are not allowed below 5 degrees Celsius.

Work is expected to resume in March 2025.

Before the bridge can reopen to traffic, a static load test is required by the city authority. The load test is scheduled after the girder replacement is finished. The project status is Pending.
