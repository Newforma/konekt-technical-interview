# Change Order 07 - City Bridge Renovation

Reason: heavy corrosion was found on the main steel girders after the sandblasting of span 2. The girders were not in the original survey.

Impact:

- Additional cost: 180,000 EUR (replacement of 14 girder sections).
- Additional duration: 6 weeks.

Approval: approved by the owner on 2024-11-04. The change order is signed and the work is authorised.
