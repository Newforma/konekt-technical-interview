# Near-Miss Report - Office Tower A - October 2024

On 14 October 2024 a hand tool fell from level 12 and landed in a controlled drop zone. Nobody was injured.

Cause: the tool was not secured with a lanyard and the toe board was missing on one side of the slab edge.

Corrective actions: toe boards installed on all open slab edges, lanyards made mandatory, toolbox talk held with all trades.
