# Site Report - Office Tower A - September 2024

Author: Site manager M. Duval. Project: Office Tower A (building 1, Silver rectangular tower).

## Progress

Structure has reached level 34 of 38. The concrete core is complete.

## Delay

The project is about 3 weeks behind the original schedule. There are two causes. First, the tower crane failed its periodic inspection on 2 September and was out of service for 9 days. Second, the delivery of the curtain wall panels slipped because of a supplier production issue.

## New target

The revised target for the end of the envelope work is 2025-01-15. The contractual due date of 2024-12-01 can no longer be met.
