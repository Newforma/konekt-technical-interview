# Subway Station Upgrade - Signaling Risk Register

Work on the signaling system is only allowed during night windows, from 01:00 to 04:30, when the line is closed.

Risks:

1. The new signaling equipment is waiting for vendor safety certification. Without it, installation cannot start. Probability: medium. Impact: high.
2. Only two night windows per week are available, which limits the progress.
3. Cable pulling in tunnel section T3 requires a flooding check first.

Status: Pending. The due date of 2025-01-10 is at risk.
